    solutions architect, solutions engineer, forward deployed, customer engineer, sales engineer, pre-sales, customer success

    Matching rules

    How the fields behave

    • Target titlesPrimary role-shape anchors. Use titles you would actually click.
    • Desired keywordsSoft OR-style boosts. A job can match any of them.
    • Required keywordsHard AND-style filters. Use sparingly.
    • ExclusionsHard blockers that remove jobs before rating.
    • Resume and skillsSupporting evidence, not the main search direction.
